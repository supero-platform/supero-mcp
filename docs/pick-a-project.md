# Picking a project

There are three ways to take part. Pick by how much time you have.

| You have | Do this | You will need |
|---|---|---|
| 30 minutes | Build a **Starter** brief by following [first-app.md](first-app.md) | One free project |
| An afternoon | Build an **Intermediate** brief, or your own idea | One free project |
| An hour, and you prefer fixing to starting | Fix a known gap in [someone else's app](../apps/README.md) | One free project |

## Choosing a brief

Open the [project list](../projects/README.md) and look at three columns.

- **Level.** *Starter* has one or two roles. *Intermediate* has several roles and rules about who sees what. *Advanced* keeps several organisations' data apart in one app.
- **Buildable today.** Every brief can be built in full on the free plan.
- **What it is.** Pick something you understand from real life. You will be the one judging whether the app is right, and that is much easier for a trade you know.

You do not need to ask before starting, and nobody owns a brief. Several people building the same one is welcome: the differences are interesting.

## Reading a brief

| Section | What it is for |
|---|---|
| The problem | The real situation. Use it to judge whether the app would help. |
| Who uses it | The roles. Each one signs in and sees something different. |
| What it keeps track of | The records. A starting point; the assistant may shape them differently. |
| What people do | The steps the app must support. |
| **Who can see what** | The rules. This is the part to check hardest. |
| Platform services | Ready-made parts to use, not rebuild. |
| **Done when** | Your test list. Try every line in the running app. |
| Stretch goals | Extras for after it works. |
| Starter prompt | Copy and paste this into your assistant. |

## Your own idea

Write one paragraph that answers four questions: who uses it, what each of them does, who must not see what, and how you will know it is done. Then use the prompt in the [README](../README.md#4-build-an-app). If the idea is good, turn it into a brief for others with [TEMPLATE.md](../projects/TEMPLATE.md).

## When you finish

Add the app to [community apps](../apps/README.md) with its files, a screenshot and an honest list of what does not work. You do not need to have finished every "Done when" line. Say which pass and which do not.
